Assassin's Creed: Mirage

Summary

On Assassin’s Creed: Mirage I was the sole level designer of Yasiriyah, a town in Baghdad’s Abbasiyah district, first as an intern and then on contract. I planned its streets, crowd life, rooftops and ambient guards as one living town, and built a lost book puzzle inside it. When a mosque I worked on couldn’t be entered, I proposed rooftop prayer, and it became a rule across the game.

On this page

    I joined Ubisoft Singapore as a level design intern in March 2022 and was given a whole town to plan. Yasiriyah is one of the towns in the Abbasiyah district of Baghdad, and I was its sole level designer, first as an intern and then as a contract Junior Level Designer. Each town had one level designer and one level artist; I was paired with a senior level artist.

    Landmarks such as the House of Wisdom and the Observatory were built by other teams. My work was the town between them: its streets, homes, rooftops, crowd life and ambient guards. I also worked on the Abbasiyah mosque in neighbouring Kahtabah, beside the Hammam.

    Planning a Living Town

    TL;DR — Roads, crowds, parkour and guards, planned as one town rather than five separate passes.

    As the town’s level designer I planned every layer the player moves through:

    • Road network: how people and players move through the streets.
    • Crowd life: where Baghdad’s daily life happens, and how it fills the space.
    • Parkour paths: routes across the rooftops that read clearly at a glance.
    • Living zones: pockets of everyday activity that make the town feel inhabited.
    • Ambient guards: a presence that keeps the town tense without blocking exploration.

    There were a few challenges with Mirage in terms of designing the Level. The Building Kits were way too large for Rich area which is where Yasiriyah is. The first iteration had the rooftop feeling like an elevated road it did not feel exciting or fun running through them, it was the same feedback I received when I presented it.

    My Senior Level Artist and I discussed this, and we came up with the idea to do kit bashing bearing the technical limitations applied to the buildings in mind. A lot of building such as the image above was kit bashed by me. I wanted to create a lot of alleyways and sheltered spaces that have many crowds flowing through.

    Bringing Faith to the Rooftops

    TL;DR — An underused prayer station became a rule for every town in the game.

    It started with that mosque. The player couldn’t enter it, so its prayer station had nowhere to go. That made me ask whether prayer could happen outside: on the rooftops, and under shelter in front of the mosque itself.

    My paired senior level artist and I wanted to bring more life, and new prop sets, to Yasiriyah’s rooftops. I researched whether prayer could respectfully take place outside a mosque, and found that it could, as long as it was done under shelter.

    A Hidden Book

    TL;DR — A lost book puzzle tucked into a house near the Observatory.

    I also built the lost book puzzle in a house west of the Observatory. The door is boarded up; inside, a bookshelf hides the stairs to the floor where the book waits.
