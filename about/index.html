My story

I'm the designer who asks
“what if we tried this?”

— and then builds it to find out. Four years and three AAA titles in, I still love the same thing: solving problems from every angle, whether it's code, art or how a player moves through a space. I design from the player's side first, path by path, and I bring my team along with prototypes, not just pitches.

— Jade
